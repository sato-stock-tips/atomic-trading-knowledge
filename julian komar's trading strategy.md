
This page gives you a clean, honest overview of how Julian trade. No theory. No fluff. Just the components I use every single day to find, enter, manage, and hold winning stocks. This is a trend-following, growth-focused process built on experience and repetition.

1. **Stock Screener & Selection:** I start with a stock screener and focus on high-growth, high-momentum stocks. Very often these stocks are up **50–100% from the 52-week low** and moved in a clean, explosive way. I only want leaders with high [[relative strength]]
    
2. **Themes & Stories:** I trade the hottest themes in the market. Capital flows into themes, not single stocks. I prefer innovative or disruptive stories that can attract large amounts of money.
    
3. **Fundamental Growth:** I look for **sales growth of 20%+ YoY** and / or **EPS growth of 50%+ YoY**. This supports sustained trends and institutional demand.
    
4. **Clear Uptrends & Accumulation:** Every stock must be in a clean uptrend with strong accumulation and high activity. Quiet, choppy stocks are ignored.
    
5. **[[market trend model]]:** I mainly trade when my market trend model is **green** and the market is in a confirmed uptrend. If the market is not aligned, I reduce risk or stay in cash. [[when not to trade]]
    
6. **Breakout Entries:** Around **90% of my trades are breakouts**. I wait for volatility contraction and very tight price action before entering.
    
7. **Staged Position Building:** I enter in steps: **50% on the breakout, 30% 1–2% higher**, and **20% another 1–2% higher**. I add only to strength.
    
8. **Position Size:** A full position is usually **10–15% of my capital**, adjusted to market conditions and [[setups]] quality.
    
9. **[[stop loss]] Placement:** I use tight stops, usually **3–5%**, placed inside the base or below the day's low. In high [[ATR%]] stocks, **7–10%** is acceptable.
    
10. **Trade Management:** I manage trades using the **EMA10 or EMA20** as trailing stops. My goal is to stay in strong trends as long as price allows.
    
11. **Partial Profits:** In fast-moving stocks, I sell **10–20% into strength** on days when the stock is up **10% or more** to reduce pressure.
    
12. **Core Objective:** I am a trend follower. My goal is to catch stocks that move **50–100% within a few weeks**. A few strong winners make the difference.
