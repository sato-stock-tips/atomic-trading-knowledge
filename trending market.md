
This trading style is only effective when the market is trending. When the Nasdaq is strong and pushing higher, traders can be aggressive, but when it is not, the market will penalize traders regardless of [[setups]] quality.

### **The 12-Step Framework**

1. **Clear Uptrend:** Momentum trading requires a solid uptrend where the Nasdaq is above a rising EMA21.
    
2. **Higher Highs & Higher Lows:** A genuine uptrend must print higher highs and [[higher lows]]; if the index continues to make new lows, trades will fail repeatedly.
    
3. **Market Breadth Matters:** Because 80% of stocks follow the general market, even the strongest growth names are hit when the market is weak.
    
4. **EMA20 as a Simple Trend Filter:** Trading above a rising EMA21 indicates high odds for momentum trading, while trading below it necessitates caution.
    
5. **[[market trend model]]:** Use a market trend model to identify clean trend phases.
    
6. **Strong Leadership:** Momentum is effective when real leaders appear, characterized by stocks hitting new highs, high relative strength, and heavy [[institutional accumulation]].
    
7. **Weak Markets Kill Momentum:** Momentum dries up, breakouts fail, and strength is sold if the Nasdaq dips below the EMA20 or if the EMA20 flattens.
    
8. **Corrections Require Patience:** Sitting tight is the best edge during corrections; momentum cannot be forced and will return on its own.
    
9. **Sideways Markets Are Dangerous:** Most traders lose more capital in sideways markets than in corrections due to chopped entries and hit stops with no follow-through.
    
10. **Trade With the Environment:** Traders do not decide if momentum works; the market does, and the trader's job is to align with it.
    
11. **[[go to cash]] Is a Position:** Protecting capital by staying in cash when the Nasdaq is not in a clean uptrend keeps a trader sharp and unemotional until a trend returns.
    
12. **Re-Enter When Strength Returns:** Once the Nasdaq regains the EMA20 and leadership stocks push to new highs, it is time to re-enter the market.
