This page explains the core idea behind my trading approach: I only look for asymmetric bets. I want to risk very little capital while giving myself the chance to make a meaningful impact on my overall account. This is not about being right often. It's about structuring trades so that one winner can change everything.

---

### **The 11-Step Framework**

1. **Asymmetric Thinking:** I’m not interested in trades where I risk 1% to make 1% or 2%. I look for situations where I risk 0.3–0.5% of my total capital to make 10%, 15%, 20% or more on the account level. Small risk, large upside.
    
2. **Tight Stop Losses:** Asymmetry only works with tight stops. Most of my stops are in the 3–5% range. In special cases, 7–10% is acceptable, but wider than that usually breaks the math. The tighter the stop, the better the risk profile. Avoid [[wide stops]]
    
3. **High-Potential Stocks Only:** This approach only works in stocks that can move far. I focus on names that can realistically move 50%, 100%, 150% or even 200% within a relatively short time. Slow, low-volatility stocks don’t qualify. [[five star setups]] only
    
4. **Clear Catalyst:** Big moves need a reason. I want a strong catalyst in the background: explosive EPS growth, massive sales growth, or a powerful story that attracts institutional capital. No catalyst, no asymmetric bet.
    
5. **Precise Entries:** I don’t buy randomly. I enter at points where the chart allows tight risk control. Clean bases, clear adherence to [[setups]], and defined invalidation points are mandatory. If I can’t define risk clearly, I skip the trade if its a [[weak setup]]
    
6. **Small Initial Risk:** I start very small. The initial position carries minimal risk; small [[R-multiples]]. This protects my account and gives me flexibility. Early entries are about information, not aggression.
    
7. **[[add position]] Only if Right:** I only increase exposure if the stock moves in my direction. Never add to losers. Risk can expand slightly once the trade proves itself, but the initial damage is always capped.
    
8. **One Winner Changes Everything:** A single trade that delivers a 10% gain on my total account can wipe out 20 small losses in a row and still leave profits. That’s the power of asymmetry.
    
9. **Low Hit Rate Is Fine:** I don’t need a high win rate. Even with a 30–40% hit rate, this approach can work extremely well. Losses are small and controlled. Winners are allowed to grow.
    
10. **Extreme Risk-Reward Focus:** I constantly look for risk-reward ratios of 5:1, 10:1, 20:1, 50:1 or more. That’s the benchmark. If the upside doesn’t clearly dwarf the downside, I’m not interested.
    
11. **Not for Everyone:** This style is emotionally demanding. Many small losses, long waiting periods, and rare big winners. It’s not comfortable. But it’s a proven way to survive tough markets and still grow capital.
