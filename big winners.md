One big winner pays for everything

Finding stocks is easy. I run a scanner and see dozens of names that fit my criteria every single day. That’s not the hard part. The hardest part of trading is staying in the big winners.

1) I don’t struggle with losses. I’m wrong 60–70% of the time. That’s normal. Small losses are part of the job.
   
2) What hurts is killing a winner too early. Selling because I get scared. Taking profits to “feel safe.” Breaking my own rules.
   
3) One real winner can wipe out months of small losses. That’s how this game works. Not by being right often — but by holding when it counts.
   
4) Trading is not an easy game. It’s mentally demanding. Staying calm while a position grows is harder than taking a loss.
   
5) I fight for my winners. I protect them. I manage them. I sit through pullbacks. I let the trend do the work. This is what separates profitable traders from frustrated ones. These patterns repeat. I’ve taught this to thousands. You can learn it too.
