**Imitate over initiate** (added 2026-07-20, user-directed): a very strong preference is
held for imitating [[martin luk]] rather than originating trades. This is the sleeve-level
application of [[imitate before innovate]] and of the fund-level admission that
[[good enough systems beat good trading]] — the operator does not trade well enough to
freelance, so the system's job is to replicate Luk's process faithfully: his preference
for high-turnover liquid names, his strength-in-weakness entries, and his sell behavior.
See the June 2026 journal studies: [[Martin Luk preference into high turnover stocks]],
[[Martin luk strength in weakness in strength]], [[Martin Luk and Sive]].

Any Fast Book trade that cannot be traced to a Luk-style setup is out of system.
