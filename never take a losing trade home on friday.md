## 

This rule has saved me hundreds of thousands of dollars.

There is a technique I use. A lot of serious traders follow the spirit of my Weekend Rule —but very few execute religiously like I do. It’s more of a shared risk principle than a named rule.

I've been a veteran commodities trader known for strict risk management rules—and my “weekend rule” is one of my simpler but practical guidelines.

Markets close on Friday but news doesn’t stop. Over the weekend, unexpected events can happen:

- Geopolitical shocks
    
- Economic announcements
    
- Natural disasters
    
- Policy changes
    

When markets reopen (Sunday evening/Monday), prices can gap up or down sharply, skipping over stop-loss orders.

If you’re holding a position:

- Your stop-loss might not trigger at your intended level
    
- Losses can be much larger than planned
    
- Volatility is unpredictable at the open
    

This is especially dangerous in:

- Futures
    
- Forex
    
- Crypto (even though crypto trades 24/7, liquidity and sentiment shifts still matter)
    
- Leveraged trades
    

I have repeatedly advised traders on this rule as I have acted on it:

From my own trading and writing (shared in a post summarizing my principles):

“My rule for weekends is to exit all trades that on Friday are underwater.”

“Never take a losing trade home on Friday.”

This rule has saved be from many adverse weekend gaps.

Early in my career trades held with a Friday loss probably cost me more money than any other type of trade.
