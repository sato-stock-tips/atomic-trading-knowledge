Trade Philosophy
## Remember your why

Why are you trading? Mine is to provide money to support your family, to provide time to nurture yourself and your loved ones, and to provide for the freedom for me to explore our beautiful world. Take a moment to center yourself with your WHY every day before trading.

Reread this scripture every week before a trading week to remind yourself of the goals, rules, reason and results of trading

### Abundance appears when you are grateful

Before trading, take a moment to be grateful for the blessings in your life. In trading, there’s an abundance of setups, abundance of instruments to trade, abundance of profits to be made. The abundance will come when you’re ready for it.

### Belief

Your trading results reflect your beliefs about the market, not the market itself. Change your beliefs, change your results (Douglas)

I believe that I can achieve the top results in the world or at least >200% per year
