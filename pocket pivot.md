- **Blue bars**: Pocket Pivot Volumes (PPV), representing up-day volume that exceeds the highest down-day volume of the previous 10 down-days.
    
- **Pocket Pivot Volumes (PPV)**: These are considered the premier indicator of institutional accumulation. Seeing multiple PPVs within a consolidation base or in a breakout candle serves as a very bullish signal.
