

1. **Wait.** Don’t buy the gap day. I wait. Late gap entries give me proof that the stock finds support and real demand after the initial reaction. I let price confirm strength before I commit capital. Let the Market Digest the News: After the gap day, I do nothing. I observe. Strong stocks don’t collapse after the gap — they hold near the highs and absorb supply. This digestion phase is critical.
    
2. **Catalyst First:** I only trade gap ups with strong, fundamental catalysts. This includes big EPS surprises, strong sales surprises, major guidance changes, or truly game-changing news for the company or industry.
    
    
3. **Post-Gap Structure:** I want to see simple and obvious behavior. Typical structures are one to three days of consolidation, an [[inside day]], tight ranges close to the highs, or sometimes direct follow-through on the second day. This shows [[institutional accumulation]]
    
4. **Entry Rule:** My entry is rule-based and boring. I buy above the high of the gap day once price confirms strength or above an inside day or range. No anticipation, no guessing, [[don't trade emotionally]]. 
   
5. May also enter when the price action [[pullback]] to the [[gaps]] support range
    
6. **Stop Placement:** Risk is defined before the trade. Stops are placed either below the low of the breakout day or inside the consolidation range. Keep risk to 1-2%. If the stop is a [[wide stops]], I skip the trade.
    
7. **Trade Management:** Once I’m in, I manage the position like any momentum trade. I trail with EMA10 or EMA20, take partial profits by [[sell into strength]], watch for climax behavior, and let the rest run as long as structure holds.
    
8. **Market Environment:** This setup works best in strong momentum markets and hot themes where institutions are actively building positions. In choppy or weak markets, I reduce size or [[go to cash]].
    
9. **Why It Works for Part-Time Traders:** Everything about this setup is visible and plannable. Clear catalyst, clear structure, clear entry, and clear risk. You don’t need to watch every tick — you need discipline and preparation and superior #psychology 
    
10. **The Real Edge:** The edge is not the gap itself. The edge is waiting, observing, and entering only when the market confirms strength. This is how I trade gap stocks with clarity and control.
