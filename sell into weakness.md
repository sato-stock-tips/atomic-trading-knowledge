### market trend model
selling all when the [[market trend model]] dictates to sell all positions within the market (or even flip short). it doesn't matter what the individual stocks are doing because the market dictates stock prices more than the individual stock
(the % mix from William O Neil is 40% market, 30% [[sector]], 30% stock)
   
### [[stop loss]] set during entry
selling when it hits the stop loss which is adjusted to be the low of previous day during entries, or during low of previous-previous day to extend some buffer especially for stocks that have been opened recently and are doing an [[inside day]]

### undercutting the ema
selling 2/3 of the outstanding position when price goes under the 10ema and the remaining 1/3 of the outstanding position when price goes under the 20ema
