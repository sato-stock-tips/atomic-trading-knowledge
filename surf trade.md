also known as pullback to moving averages

### description of surf trade

surf trade is when a stock with tremendous [[relative strength]] takes a breather and comes back to the [[key moving averages]] that's guiding them. When a stock is having a big run-up and having good adherence to a key moving average (be it the EMA 10 or the EMA 20). 

Sometimes, the stock will take one or two sessions not continuing upwards but drifting back towards the EMA that was guiding it throughout the run-up. When the stock price drifts within one ATR multiple, one ATR percentage multiple from the moving average (be it EMA 10 for really strong stocks or EMA 20 for strong stocks), that could provide a good buying opportunity to get on to a proven, relatively strong trending stock that is moving in full momentum.

### conditions of surf trade

key condition 1 is that the price drift back to the supporting ema should also have dry up in [[volume]] seen by a drop in [[relative volume at time]]

key condition 2 with surf trade is to only enter if the stock is less than 4x [[ATR%]] from the 50 simple moving average (sma). if the [[eps & sales]] fundamentals are good then 5x ATR% from 50 sma may be tolerated. this is because we do not want to open [[extended stocks]]  and avoid [[entering stocks late]] and avoid #FOMO 

### surf trade entries

if the surf trade stock price is in a legitimate [[pullback]] then an IBKR midprice order or a market order types are preferred

if the surf trade stock price is in a [[breakout]] then [[opening range breakout]] order type is preferred
