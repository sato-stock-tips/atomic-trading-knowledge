This guide details a minimalistic volume indicator designed to remove "noise" and highlight three specific types of volume bars.

### **Core Volume Bar Definitions**

- **Blue bars**: [[pocket pivot]] Volumes (PPV), representing up-day volume that exceeds the highest down-day volume of the previous 10 down-days.
    
- **Red bars**: Down-day volumes that are higher than the 50-period moving average of the volume.
    
- **Green bars**: Up-day volumes that are higher than the 50-period moving average of the volume.
    
- **Dark grey bars**: All other volumes considered "noise" that do not require actionable consideration.
    

---

### **Actionable Insights for Long Entries**

When initiating a long entry, traders should monitor these bars for specific signals:

- **RRV (recent red volumes)**: These indicate unconsumed supply. Action is taken either when the highs of these red days are surpassed or when they are followed by higher **RGV (recent green volumes)**.
    
- **Pocket Pivot Volumes (PPV)**: These are considered the premier indicator of institutional accumulation. Seeing multiple PPVs within a consolidation base or in a breakout candle serves as a very bullish signal.
    

### **Design Philosophy**

The indicator purposely hides the volume moving average line because the dark grey bars themselves signify below-average volume. This color scheme is intended to be self-explanatory and devoid of distractions, allowing traders to avoid comparing every bar to an overlying line.
