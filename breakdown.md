A breakdown occurs when price violates key [[support & resistance]] levels and [[mark minervini (key moving averages)]], signaling institutional distribution and potential trend reversal.

## What is a Breakdown?

A breakdown is the opposite of a [[breakout]]. It's when price decisively breaks below critical support levels with volume, indicating that supply has overwhelmed demand and the prior uptrend is likely over.

**Critical distinction:** Not every dip below support is a breakdown. A true breakdown shows institutional selling, not just retail shakeouts.

## Breakdown Signals (from Qullamaggie)

### Primary Breakdown Triggers

**1. Breaking EMA 10 on High Volume**
- Price closes below EMA 10 with [[relative volume at time]] >1.2
- This is your first warning - per [[sell into weakness]], sell 2/3 of position
- The faster and stronger stocks typically respect EMA 10 as support
- When EMA 10 fails with volume, institutions are exiting

**2. Breaking EMA 20 on Any Volume**
- Price closes below EMA 20
- Per [[sell into weakness]], sell remaining 1/3 of position
- EMA 20 is the final line of defense for healthy trends
- Once EMA 20 breaks, the trend structure is compromised

**3. Breaking SMA 50 with Momentum Failure**
- Price breaks below SMA 50 after multiple attempts to hold
- Usually accompanied by negative weekly performance
- This signals the intermediate trend is broken
- All positions should be exited before this point per rules above

### Secondary Breakdown Patterns

**4. Failed Gap Pattern**
- Stock gaps up (>2%) but sells off hard from open (>3% down from open)
- High [[relative volume at time]] on the selloff (>1.5)
- **This is distribution disguised as strength**
- Example: Gap +4.5% but close -3.5% from open = 8% reversal
- Institutions use gap strength to distribute shares to retail

**5. Volume Climax Reversal**
- Massive up day (+7-10%+) on very high volume (>2.0 Rel Vol)
- Followed immediately by down day with elevated volume
- This is a [[pocket pivot]] that failed - sell per [[sell into strength]]
- The climax exhausts all buyers, leaving only sellers

**6. Breaking Key Price Levels**
- Violation of prior swing lows from the consolidation base
- Breaking below [[bjorgum key levels]]
- These are visible levels institutions use for [[stop loss]] placement
- When broken, cascade of stops triggers accelerated selling

## Volume Patterns in Breakdowns

**Distribution Volume Profile:**
- High volume on down days
- Low volume on up days (weak bounces)
- [[relative volume at time]] consistently >1.0 on red candles
- This is opposite of accumulation ([[institutional accumulation]])

**Volume Dry-Up is Bullish, Not Bearish:**
- Don't confuse low volume dips with breakdowns
- [[surf trade]] entries require volume dry-up on pullbacks
- True breakdowns show INCREASED volume as support breaks

## Extension Breakdowns

From Qullamaggie's framework:

**When a stock is extended >4× [[ATR%]] from SMA 50:**
- Any support break is more dangerous
- Risk/reward has deteriorated significantly
- Bounces are often selling opportunities, not buy points
- These are [[extended stocks]] that should have been sold per [[7-11 take profit rule]]

**Example:**
```
Stock at 5.5× ATR% from SMA 50
Breaks EMA 10 on volume
= IMMEDIATE EXIT, no second chances
Extended + breakdown = fast reversals coming
```

## Psychological Aspects (Qullamaggie)

- "It will bounce back like last time"
- "I'll sell on the next rally"

**Reality:**
- Most breakdowns accelerate after initial support break
- The best bounce you'll get is often the first one
- Every day you hold, you're compounding a mistake

**Professional Response:**
- Respect the breakdown signal
- Exit immediately per rules
- Don't try to predict if it's "real" or a "fake-out"
- [[price is the best information]] - the chart is telling you to exit

## Breakdown vs. Shakeout

**How to Tell the Difference:**

**Breakdown characteristics:**
- High volume on the break
- Closes near lows of the day
- Fails to reclaim support quickly (within 1-2 sessions)
- Happens after [[extended stocks]] positioning
- Multiple MA violations in succession

**[[shakeout]] characteristics:**
- Quick violation with immediate reversal same day
- Forms long lower wick (tests support, buyers step in)
- Low volume on the break
- Reclaims support by close or next session
- Happens when NOT extended (<3× ATR% from SMA 50)

**When in doubt, follow your rules:**
- If price breaks EMA 10 on volume, sell 2/3 per [[sell into weakness]]
- Don't gamble on whether it's a shakeout
- You can always re-enter if it reclaims, but you can't un-lose money

## The Cascade Effect

Qullamaggie teaches that breakdowns often accelerate:

**Phase 1: First Support Break (EMA 10)**
- Smart money exits
- Retail holds "waiting for bounce"

**Phase 2: Second Support Break (EMA 20)**
- More institutional selling
- Retail starts to panic
- You should be completely out by now

**Phase 3: Final Capitulation (SMA 50)**
- Retail finally gives up
- Maximum pain and volume
- If you're still holding here, you violated all rules

**The key:** Exit in Phase 1, never wait for Phase 2 or 3.

## Recovery Timeline

Per Qullamaggie, broken stocks need time to repair:

**After a true breakdown:**
- Stock typically needs 3-6 weeks minimum to form new base
- Multiple tests of new support required
- Need to see [[institutional accumulation]] return
- New [[breakout]] setup must form before re-entry

**Don't try to catch falling knives:**
- Let it complete the breakdown
- Let it build a new base
- Wait for proper [[surf trade]], [[turf trade]], or [[rebirth trade]] setup
- There's always another opportunity

## Integration with Trading System

**Breakdown Prevention:**
- Never buy [[extended stocks]] (>4× ATR% from SMA 50)
- Always use [[stop loss]] placement below support
- Follow [[sell into weakness]] rules mechanically
- Respect [[sell into strength]] on climax moves

**Breakdown Response:**
- Exit per [[sell into weakness]]: 2/3 when breaks EMA 10, 1/3 when breaks EMA 20
- Don't average down ([[add position]] only works on winners, never losers)
- Don't hope for recovery
- Protect capital per [[small losses protect equity curve]]

**Post-Breakdown:**
- Mark the stock for watchlist
- Wait for new base formation (3-6 weeks minimum)
- Look for [[rebirth trade]] setup if fundamentals still strong
- Most importantly: move on to new opportunities
