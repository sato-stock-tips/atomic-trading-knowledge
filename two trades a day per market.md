### two trades a day

this applies to OPENING a maximum of two trades a day. does not apply to already open stocks

Spread out your trades because most positions are correlated. Having too many trades open on the same day increases the risk of all of them suffering together on an adverse day.

Look through all your potential trade opens and identify the best one and only open that best two. This ensures that you know how much new risk you're putting into the market per day and this allows you to size the position appropriately.
