7 Signs you're trading emotionally and not following the system

1. **Impulse Entries:** You buy because the chart moves — not because it fits your [[setup]]. Emotion: fear of missing out. Result: weak entries and random outcomes.
    
2. **Random Size:** You size bigger when confident, smaller when scared. That’s not risk management — that’s mood management.
    
3. **Revenge Trades:** After a stop-out, you jump back in to “get it back.” It’s not a strategy — it’s a stress reaction that compounds losses.
    
4. **Confirmation Scrolling:** You search Twitter or Discord for someone who agrees with you. That’s not analysis — that’s validation-seeking.
    
5. **Exit Anxiety:** You sell winners too early because you’re scared to lose unrealized gains. Fear replaces rules — and profits vanish.
    
6. **Avoidance:** You skip journaling losing trades. You tell yourself “I already know the mistake.” But without review, you’ll repeat it again.
    
7. **Hope Mode:** You move your stop “just a little more.” That’s not flexibility — that’s denial. Hope doesn’t protect capital.
   
8. FOMO can take over in highly trending markets. there are rules such as [[two trades a day per market]] to rein in these human emotions
