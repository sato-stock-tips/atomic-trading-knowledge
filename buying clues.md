**12 Signs a Chart is “Trade-Ready”** — underlying principle: [[price is the best information]]

Not every nice-looking chart is worth your money. Before I commit capital, I want to see a combination of structure, momentum, and market context — including [[eps & sales]] for fundamental fuel. Here are 12 signs that a chart is ready for a high-probability trade.

**[Chart Annotation]**

- **We bought this 5 stars setup here!**
    

1. **Clear Uptrend:** Price is above EMA 20 and EMA 50, both sloping upward.
    
2. **Strong [[relative strength]]:** [[RS rating]] >90 or clearly outperforming index.
    
3. **Tight Price Action:** Recent bars show reduced volatility after a strong run. [[three small bros]]
    
4. **Defined Pattern:** Flat base, flag, or VCP — no messy ranges or random spikes.
    
5. **[[volume]] Clues:** At least one or two up-days with 1.5–2x average volume in recent weeks.
    
6. **Light Pullbacks:** Down days on low [[volume]] — no aggressive selling. Price tends to pop upwards after days of super low volume.
    
7. **Liquidity:** $100M+ average daily turnover for easy entry and exit.
    
8. **Catalyst Potential:** Earnings, news, or [[industry (previously known as sectors)]] momentum could drive a breakout.
    
9. **[[ATR%]] in Range:** 5%+ average true range — enough movement without excessive risk.
    
10. **Sector or Theme Strength:** The stock's group or theme is also trending higher.
    
11. **No [[extended stocks]]:** Price is within 5% of the [setup], not stretched far above.
    
12. **Market Alignment:** Overall [[market trend model]] is positive, reducing headwinds.

## **7 Clear Warning Signs — When a Stock Is NOT Ready Yet**

Sometimes the best trade is the one you don't take! Too many traders waste time and capital on mediocre setups that never had real potential. The key is knowing when to step aside.

**[Chart Annotations]**

- **Clear downtrend, choppy, no momentum, no story, no potential = Avoid!!**
    
- **Strong momentum, clean upmoves, tight patterns, high-potential story = Trade!**
    
- **We bought here!**
    

1. **Loose Price Action:** Wild, unpredictable candles with long wicks and no consistent trend? That’s not strength — it’s noise. If price action looks erratic, it often means institutions aren’t involved yet. I wait until the chart tightens up.
    
2. **No [[volume]] Support:** Volume is the fuel behind every move. If a stock breaks out with no increase in volume, that’s not conviction — it’s a fakeout waiting to happen. I want to see 1.5x–2x average volume on up days before I act.
    
3. **Below Key EMAs:** If the price is still under EMA 20 or even EMA 10, it’s too early. Strong stocks trade above rising moving averages. If a base forms below those levels, the momentum just isn't there yet.
    
4. **Failed Breakouts in the Chart History:** A failed breakout is a red flag. Two failed breakouts? That’s often a dealbreaker. Repeated failures show that buyers lack control — and sellers are stepping in. I don’t force trades into that kind of chart history.
    
5. **No Sector or Theme Tailwind:** Even a good chart will struggle in a weak sector. If the entire theme — like EVs, data centers, or drones — is under pressure, I avoid trading it. Sector momentum is 30% of the move according to William O Neil.
    
6. **No Compelling Story or [[institutional accumulation]]:** I want stocks that funds want to own — and that requires a story: strong earnings, new technology, clear growth narrative. If there’s no EPS growth, no theme, and no interest in the news flow, I pass.
    
7. **Choppy or Deep Base Structure:** If a base is wide, deep, and shows no volatility contraction — it’s not a real setup. I need clean structures: flags, flat bases, VCP. Choppy consolidations show indecision, not readiness.
