### on individual stocks

7-11 take profit rule refers to the stock price [[ATR%]] multiple away from the 50 SMA. The rule **only activates when the stock reaches 7x ATR% above the 50-day SMA**. It does NOT apply at 4x, 5x, or 6x — those are extension warning zones but not 7-11 profit-taking zones.

**The 7-11 zone is specifically 7x through 11x ATR% above the SMA 50. Nothing below 7x triggers this rule.**

When the stock is at 7 x ATR percentage from the 50-day SMA, sell 20% of the current position size. Do the same at 8x, 9x, 10x, and 11x — take profit at each mark for 20% of the current size.

Example: a stock with ATR% of 5% and SMA 50 at $100
- 7x = $135 → sell 20% (first trim)
- 8x = $140 → sell 20%
- 9x = $145 → sell 20%
- 10x = $150 → sell 20%
- 11x = $155 → sell 20%
- At 5x ($125) or 6x ($130) → **do NOT sell under this rule**

After selling five times at 7-11, there should remain only around one-third the position left (as 0.8^5 = 0.32)
