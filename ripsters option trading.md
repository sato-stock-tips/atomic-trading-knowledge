
RIPSTER'S OPTIONS TRADING RULES
 * Do not trade or take any trades first 10 minutes at open. Let option prices settle down and price action confirm.
 * I Buy 2 ITM or 3 OTM from current price. Exception is higher priced stocks like AMZN, GOOGL but in that case only trade such names on Thursday/Friday. I prefer ITM or 1 OTM for swings.
 * Avoid Spready Options or Stocks with very low Average Option Volume. Mostly Stocks with Weeklies are preferred. Less than 10-20 cents spread is preferred; 1 to 5 cent is best.
 * Choose stocks with high [[ATR%]] and relatively low float for large cap for quick and big moves for best R/R. Option Strike Price & Delta are usually assigned accordingly. This also means that Options can move against you fast.
 * Prefer Options priced between 20 Cents to 3 Dollar. Rule # 2 must work in conjunction with this rule.
 * Don't take size more than your per trade risk [[R-multiples]]. Divide size as per your risk/ [[stop loss]]. Huge Option Sizes on average account size is road to blowing up it all.
 * Always cut your option trade if it is goes against you twice.
 * Low to Average IV Options are preferred. Never overnight high IV Option Contracts
 * Always Puts at Resistance and Calls at Support. Never chase the trend, let price pull back before your execute your trade.
 * NEVER Average Down (unless it is preplanned Scale In - conviction trade). Emotional Average Down works sometime but burns big in the long run. Also decay kills you
