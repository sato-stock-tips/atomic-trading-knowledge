Keeping your LOSSES tiny is the real EDGE in trading. Most traders chase winners — I focus on protecting my equity curve. That’s why I’ve survived 20 years in this game. Here’s how I manage risk 

1) Small Stops Only I keep my stops in the 3–5% range. In rare cases 7–10%. If a trade needs more room, it’s usually not a strong setup. Tight, precise, focused — that’s the key. 
   
2) Multiple Attempts Beat One Big Loss I’d rather get stopped out four times with tiny losses than take one big hit with a wide stop. Small cuts keep me alive. Big losses destroy your month. 
   
3) Risk Less — Not More Most traders risk 1% per trade. I rarely do that. I risk 0.2–0.6% of my equity per attempt. Less stress, less damage, more confidence. It’s better to try multiple times with small size than load up once and hope.  [[R-multiples]]
   
4) Small Losses Protect Your Mind If you keep losses tiny, you can trade the next setup with a clear head. No revenge. No fear. You stay in the game long enough to catch the outlier winners. 
   
5) Avoid Cluster Risk Five AI data center stocks are one single position — not five. Correlation kills you. I’d rather own one strong leader with small defined risk instead of spreading risk across identical themes. 
   
6) The Goal: Stability Small losses keep the equity curve stable. Stable equity builds confidence. Confidence allows you to execute your system clean. This is how I trade. This is how I stay in the game. And these patterns repeat — I’ve taught this to thousands of traders. You can learn it too
