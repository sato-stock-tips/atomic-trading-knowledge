## 10 signs of a weak [[setups]]

1. **Loose Price Action:** Wide, sloppy swings instead of tight ranges signal no control.
    
2. **Declining [[relative strength]]:** Stock lags peers or indices — strength should lead, not follow.
    
3. **Weak Volume Pattern:** More down days with heavy volume than up days.
    
4. **No Clear Pivot:** Entry level is vague — if you can’t define it, skip it.
    
5. **Extended From Base:** Already 5%+ above a clean base, leaving no safe stop.
    
6. **Overcrowded Theme:** Too many weak names moving together without real leaders.
    
7. **Bad Earnings or Sales Trend:** Flat or negative EPS or sales growth won’t attract institutions.
    
8. **Thin Liquidity:** Low average turnover makes it easy to get trapped.
    
9. **No [[institutional accumulation]]:** Lack of accumulation spikes or support at key levels.
    
10. **Market Out of Sync:** Stock may look good, but the overall [[market trend model]] is against you.
