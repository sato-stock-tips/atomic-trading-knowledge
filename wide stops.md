**8 Traps of Wide Stops**

1. **False Safety**: Wide stops don't protect you — they just hide losses longer.
    
2. **Risk Creep**: A 15% stop with 10% size = oversized risk.
    
3. **Hope Trades**: Wide stops turn small mistakes into account killers.
    
4. **No Cushion**: You can't size properly when risk balloons.
    
5. **Emotional Spiral**: Watching a stock sink 20% drains conviction.
    
6. **No Rule Discipline**: Wide stops = no process. Just wishful thinking.
    
7. **Scaling Impossible**: You can't add when risk is already maxed.
    
8. **Survivorship Bias**: Stories of "big winners held through 40% drawdowns" ignore the graveyard of failures.
