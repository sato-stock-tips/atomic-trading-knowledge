

### continuation patterns
- Symmetrical Triangles as continuations: volume contraction then expansion (Schabacker)
- Right-Angle Triangles: ascending in uptrends, descending in downtrends (Schabacker)
- Rectangles as continuations: volume patterns (Schabacker)
- Flags and Pennants: measurement, relation to wedges (Schabacker)
- Head-and-Shoulders as continuations (Schabacker)
- Inverted Triangles as continuations (Schabacker)
- Patterns that change nature from reversal to continuation (Schabacker)
- Consolidation Formations: flags, pennants, rectangles in ongoing trends (Edwards & Magee)
