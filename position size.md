

Most traders obsess about entries. But it's not the entry that decides your survival—it's the size of your position. Sizing determines whether you can sit through volatility, avoid blow-ups, and compound steadily. Here's why it matters and how to master it.

| **Metric**           | **Guidance**                      |
| -------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| **Risk Per Trade**   | Max 0.25% of capital              |
| **Initial Size**     | 10–15% of capital                 |
| **Max Position Cap** | 30–35% of capital                 |
| **Scaling**          | Add 50% then 25% only on strength |

---

### **The 10-Step Framework**

1. **Risk Per Trade [[R-multiples]]:** Never risk more than 0.30% of total capital. This keeps any single trade from derailing your equity curve. I mostly risk less than 0.25% of my capital!
    
2. **Initial Position Size:** Start 10–15% of capital in a trade. This is big enough to matter but small enough to manage.
    
3. **Max Position Cap:** Limit even your strongest conviction to 30–35% of capital. Anything above is gambling, not structure.
    
4. **Scaling Rules:** Add only if the stock confirms strength—never while it's sinking. Each add should be 10–20% of the original size.
    
5. **Stop Placement:** Sizing must match your stop. A tighter stop = bigger size. Wider stop = smaller size.
    
6. **Drawdown Protection:** Smaller positions reduce stress during losing streaks and help you stick to the system.
    
7. **Conviction Builds Over Time:** Big size is earned. You size up when the trend proves itself with clean EMAs and volume support.
    
8. **Avoid Roundtrips:** Without proper sizing, small winners can turn into big losers. A correct size prevents emotional chaos.
    
9. **Compounding Effect:** Position sizing isn't about one trade. It's about surviving long enough to let winners multiply and losers stay small.
    
10. **Professional Mindset:** Pros don't ask, "What can I make?" They ask, "What can I lose?" That shift is what keeps them in the game.
