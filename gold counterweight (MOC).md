# Gold Counterweight

> Style: **hedge / counterweight** ([[multi strategy fund]]). 

## Thesis
A non-correlated hard-asset position that cushions the equity sleeves during regime stress and expresses the dollar-debasement / hard-asset view. Sizing is about portfolio insurance, not alpha.

## Operating loop (MO)
1. **Source** — gold (and adjacent hard assets) instruments
2. **Filter** — vehicle choice (physical proxy / ETF / miners)
3. **Allocate** — counterweight sizing vs. total equity exposure
4. **Enter** — accumulate; add on equity-sleeve risk-on extremes
5. **Manage** — rebalance against equity sleeves
6. **Exit / review** — trim when equity sleeves are washed out and cheap

## Entry rule
When gold is positive on the 6/20 MACD weekly (green histogram), long 10% of portfolio in gold, with the allocation coming out of [[Momentum Discretion (MOC)|Momentum Discretion]].
