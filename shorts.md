shorts can be entered in a confirmed downtrend
a confirmed downtrend is identified by

1) price is below 10ema and 20ema and
2) the 10ema is under the 20 ema
3) and the 20ema is under the 50sma
4) and the price is under the 50sma as well as 200sma
5) alignment of [[key moving averages]] in reverse from long (ie. 200sma, 150sma, 50sma, 20ema, 10ema)
6) in a losing [[industry (previously known as sectors)]]
7) appearance of [[distribution]] days
8) [[breakdown]]
9) red candles following [[custom candle stage analysis]]

often, key ideas used in looking for longs can also be flipped in direction (but not in philosophy) to look for shorts

for example,
1. months of [[consolidation]], ending with a [[big base breakout]] to the downtrend
2. [[tight base]] and then trending to the downside
3. weak [[relative strength]] and [[RS rating]]
4. [[vcp]] and dry up of [[volume]]
5. looking for [[surf trade]] and [[turf trade]] only to the downside as the only difference
