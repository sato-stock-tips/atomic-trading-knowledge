_Every pro trader knows: it’s not the losses that destroy accounts — it’s the hesitation before taking them. Cutting fast means staying liquid, focused, and emotionally stable._

### **The 12-Step Framework**

1. **Pre-Defined Risk:** Know your exit before entry. Max. 7-10% [[stop loss]]!
    
2. **No Debate Zone:** Once stop hits — you’re out. Period.
    
3. **No Averaging Down:** Conviction without data = hope.
    
4. **Micro-Losses:** Keep avg. losses $\leq 1R$. You can’t recover from 5R hits easily.
    
5. **Volume Confirmation:** Exit fast when volume confirms weakness.
    
6. **Avoid “It’ll Bounce” Thinking:** That’s emotion, not edge.
    
7. **Re-Entry Clarity:** Being wrong doesn’t mean staying out forever.
    
8. **Detach Ego:** The faster you cut, the faster you regain objectivity.
    
9. **System Trust:** A small loss executed correctly is a _win for discipline_.
    
10. **No Revenge Trades:** Reset fully before next setup.
    
11. **Review Mistake Type:** Emotional or structural? Fix accordingly.
    
12. **Consistency Rule:** Fast cuts keep equity curves smooth and confidence high.
