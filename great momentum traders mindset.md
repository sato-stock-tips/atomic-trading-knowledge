_Momentum Masters captures a rare roundtable between four of the most successful stock traders of our time — Mark Minervini, David Ryan, Dan Zanger, and Mark Ritchie II._ _Their conversation strips away the noise and exposes what truly separates professionals from amateurs — discipline, structure, and patience._

This is a supplement to [[My Trading Mindset]]

### **The 10 Lessons**

1. **Follow the Price, Not Your Opinion:** "You want to be neutral and agnostic. **Trust the charts and your research, and buy the stocks that meet your criteria.** Your opinion will always cost you money in the long run." Momentum trading isn't about predicting — it's about reacting. Let price and volume guide you, not forecasts or feelings.
    
2. **Trade With the Trend:** "I look at the chart and the long-term trend first, because I'm not going to buy a stock even if it has good fundamentals if the chart is bad and the stock is in a downtrend." **Strong charts lead strong stocks.** The best traders align with direction — never against it.
    
3. **Price Action Is Everything:** "**Price action is everything to me.** Show me the big movers, and I'll show you a stock I want to own." Momentum lives in price behavior — not in opinions, news, or narratives. Leaders reveal themselves through strength and persistence.
    
4. **Risk Small When the [[setups]] Is Late:** "If you have to buy one of these late-stage bases, buy small positions so as not to get pummeled if they fail." Discipline means sizing according to risk. **[[entering stocks late]] demand smaller exposure and faster exits** if proven wrong.
    
5. **Protect First, Profit Second:** "The key to success is not how much you make, but how little you lose when you're wrong because [[small losses protect equity curve]]" Every top performer builds longevity through capital protection. **Risk control isn't optional — it's the edge itself.**
    
6. **Develop 'Sit-Out Power':** "**You must develop sit-out power** — the ability to wait for correct setups and not force action or take subpar trades." Patience is a position. The best trades come to those who wait. Forcing activity destroys edge and energy.
    
7. **Focus on Earnings Acceleration:** "You can't fake earnings." Momentum thrives when fundamentals confirm strength. **Explosive EPS and sales growth attract the big money** — nothing else.
    
8. **Build Around [[relative strength]], Not Hope:** "If you are buying stocks that have previously opened on huge down [[gaps]] over the past three to six months, you could be taking a much higher amount of risk by owning." **Avoid damaged charts and hope trades.** True leaders don't need rescuing — they're already outperforming.
    
9. **Exposure Must Match Market Health:** **Momentum strategies work only when the market supports them.** When indexes weaken, reduce exposure. [[when not to trade]] Cash is a position — and often the strongest one.
    
10. **Master the Feedback Loop:** "Trading is a feedback loop — learn or repeat." Review every trade, refine your process, and keep adapting. **The real growth comes from studying your own behavior**, not just the market's.
