Here’s what I focus on when I wait for the real five-star [[setups]] 
1) Strong Momentum First I want stocks that already moved +50% to +100%. Real strength shows itself early. Weak names don’t magically become leaders. 
   
2) Tight Consolidation The best setups calm down after the run. A tight 2–3 week base is great. A tight 4–5 week base is even better. Volatility contracts before the next leg. 
   
3) Perfect Structure No noise. No wide ranges. No sloppy candles. A clean range with clear upper resistance tells me institutions are absorbing supply. 
   
4) Big Volume in the Move Up The prior run must show heavy demand. Multiple up-days on strong volume. If funds weren’t buying earlier, they won’t magically appear at the breakout. 
   
5) Catalyst in the Background Earnings, guidance, [[sector]] strength, big story. Momentum needs fuel. I want a reason why buyers will stay aggressive. 
   
6) larger bases after the strong initial momentum can be very constructive for a [[big base breakout]] . as they say, the bigger the base, the higher in space (ie. [[big winners]] come from tight constructive big bases)
   
7) Prepared and Ready Your screening work matters. Your watchlists matter. When the five-star setup shows up, you can’t hesitate. You must be ready to execute immediately. The truth? These setups don’t come often — but when they come, they push your equity curve to new highs. Learn to wait. That’s where the real money is.
   
8) [[upcoming earnings date]] is more than 7 days away

### **The 8-Step Framework**

1. **Prior Power Move:** The stock must be up **70% or more** in the last advance. $NB was up 136%! Big breakouts only work if institutions already proved their interest with real price movement.
    
2. **Clean, Straight Advance:** The prior upmove has to be strong and direct. Either a powerful gap with follow-through or a persistent, smooth push higher. No choppy price action, no overlapping candles. Leaders move with clarity.
    
3. **Tight Consolidation:** After the run, price must compress. Daily ranges shrink, volatility contracts, candles tighten. This is where weak hands get shaken out and strong hands stay in control. Volume dryup is a big plus too!
    
4. **Visible Accumulation:** Volume confirms everything. I want to see strong volume on up-days and quiet pullbacks. Heavy selling inside the base disqualifies the setup immediately.
    
5. **Momentum Context:** These setups work best in a risk-on environment. I want momentum expansion and leadership behavior. Strong stocks stay near highs while the rest of the market hesitates. [[market trend model]]
    
6. **Real Catalyst:** A 5-star breakout needs fuel. Either **sales +20% YoY, EPS +50% YoY**, or a clear structural story like rare earth materials in case of $NB.
    
7. **Obvious Breakout Level:** The breakout line must be clear and well tested. A level price respected multiple times. If I have to explain where resistance is, it's not clean enough.
    
8. **Tight Risk Management:** I buy the breakout with a precise entry and place a tight stop just below the base or breakout level. If it fails, I'm out. Small risk, open upside — that's the entire edge. [[assymetric bets]] [[R-multiples]]
