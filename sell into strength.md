_no round-tripping_

Selling into strength is one of the hardest skills in trading. It goes directly against greed, hope, and the dream of catching the exact top. I learned this the hard way — by watching strong winners turn into frustrating roundtrips.

We have three sell into strengths rules

### 1. selling one-third of position 3-5 days after open

According to our mantra, which is to buy stocks that will go up the fastest with the least possible risk, we should be timing their entries well. If done correctly, there should be an immediate pump after entry. This also correlates with [[dan zanger technical trading rules]] philosophy of holding on to stocks that are pumping and letting go of stocks that are not.

If we entered at the right time, we should be able to sell one-third of the position three to five days after open at a profit. This would increase our overall P&L and remove risk from our book. That lowers opportunity cost for the trader

### 2. 69 take profit rule on individual stocks

By closing 1/3 of the position after 3-5 days of opening the position, the rest of the position is subjected to the [[69 take profit rule]]

### methods to sell into strength

1. [[69 take profit rule]] on the individual stock — 20% at each ATR% multiple **starting at 7x ATR% above the 50 SMA** (not before). The 7-11 rule does not apply below 7x.

2. [[69 take profit rule]] on the market — 10% at each ATR% multiple for all stocks in that index. Same principle: only triggers at 6x+ ATR% above the 50 SMA of the market index.
   
3. **Vertical Price Action:** When a stock accelerates sharply, emotions peak and structure weakens. Fast upside moves feel great, but they increase the probability of sharp pullbacks. Vertical moves are not a gift — they are a warning to manage risk.
   
4. **[[pocket pivot]] Rule:** Take profits of 10% of the position into strength when the stock is up more than +10% on the day on a pocket pivot day. This rule is mechanical. No prediction. No discretion. Just execution.
   
5. **Climax Tops:** True climax moves are emotional, fast, and often unsustainable. They take experience to identify correctly. When I see a real climax, I sell 70–90% of the position into strength to protect the bulk of the gains. See [[exit parabolic stocks]] for the specific rules. Climax tops often coincide with broader [[distribution]] in the market or sector.

Remember that **Speed of Reversals:** Vertical moves usually don't fade slowly. They reverse fast. Stocks don't ask for permission before dropping. If you don't sell into strength, the market will force the exit — often at much worse prices.

**Selling Is a Skill:** Most traders know how to buy. Few know how to sell well. Selling into strength must be trained, repeated, and rule-based. I don't improvise exits — I follow my process.
    
**Capital Protection:** The goal is not the maximum gain on one trade. The goal is a smooth equity curve, protected capital, and mental stability. Big winners matter — but keeping them matters more. Remember that [[small losses protect equity curve]]
