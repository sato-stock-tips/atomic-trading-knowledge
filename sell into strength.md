_no round-tripping_

Selling into strength is one of the hardest skills in trading. It goes directly against greed, hope, and the dream of catching the exact top. I learned this the hard way — by watching strong winners turn into frustrating roundtrips.

### methods to sell into strength

1. [[7-11 take profit rule]] on the individual stock — 20% at each ATR% multiple **starting at 7x ATR% above the 50 SMA** (not before). The 7-11 rule does not apply below 7x.

2. [[7-11 take profit rule]] on the market — 10% at each ATR% multiple for all stocks in that index. Same principle: only triggers at 7x+ ATR% above the 50 SMA of the market index.
   
3. **Vertical Price Action:** When a stock accelerates sharply, emotions peak and structure weakens. Fast upside moves feel great, but they increase the probability of sharp pullbacks. Vertical moves are not a gift — they are a warning to manage risk.
   
4. **[[pocket pivot]] Rule:** Take profits of 10% of the position into strength when the stock is up more than +10% on the day on a pocket pivot day. This rule is mechanical. No prediction. No discretion. Just execution.
   
5. **Climax Tops:** True climax moves are emotional, fast, and often unsustainable. They take experience to identify correctly. When I see a real climax, I sell 70–90% of the position into strength to protect the bulk of the gains.

Remember that **Speed of Reversals:** Vertical moves usually don't fade slowly. They reverse fast. Stocks don't ask for permission before dropping. If you don't sell into strength, the market will force the exit — often at much worse prices.

**Selling Is a Skill:** Most traders know how to buy. Few know how to sell well. Selling into strength must be trained, repeated, and rule-based. I don't improvise exits — I follow my process.
    
**Capital Protection:** The goal is not the maximum gain on one trade. The goal is a smooth equity curve, protected capital, and mental stability. Big winners matter — but keeping them matters more. Remember that [[small losses protect equity curve]]
