How to exit a trade

Selling into strength is preferable to selling into weakness. When selling into weak price action, it usually is the case that other traders’ stops are being hit too, resulting in cascading selling pressure. Prioritizing selling into strength.

## Selling into strength

### Sell into overextension from ema

When a stock flies too high and overextends itself away from key ema, it may be time to look to sell. A good rule of thumb is to start selling when the stock is more than 5 times the average true range % (ATR%) from the 50 day moving average.
