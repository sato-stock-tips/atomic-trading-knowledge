### Trend Analysis and Trend Lines
- Definition and theory of trend lines as boundaries of price movement (Schabacker)
- Practical uses: establishing trends, signaling reversals via breaks, and estimating movement extent (Schabacker)
- Double trend lines or channels for parallel support/resistance (Schabacker)
- Fanning and flattening trends indicating weakening momentum (Schabacker)
- Major, intermediate, and minor trends with primary trend lines for long-term trading (Edwards & Magee)
- Trend channels and their breaks as entry/exit signals (Edwards & Magee)
- Throwbacks after trend breaks as retest opportunities (Schabacker)
- Trading with vs. against the major trend for higher profitability (Schabacker)

### Support and Resistance
- Definition as price levels where buying/selling pressure halts trends (Schabacker)
- Horizontal trading areas as consolidation zones forming support/resistance (Schabacker)
- Significant breaks through support/resistance with volume confirmation (Schabacker)
- Role in patterns like triangles and rectangles (Edwards & Magee)
- Psychological aspects: past highs/lows become future barriers (Edwards & Magee)
