## Unfilled gaps

One of the most bullish characteristics a stock can have is an unfilled gap below current price.

Why?

An unfilled gap tells you that buyers were so aggressive that sellers couldn't create enough supply to retrace the move. 

Instead of filling the gap, price continues to trend higher.

Strong stocks don't spend time looking back. 

They spend time building above key levels while [[institutional accumulation]] shares.

When a stock repeatedly refuses to fill a gap, it's often a sign that demand is overwhelming supply.

The strongest names in every cycle tend to leave footprints like this.

I think the gap support at the top of the gap is quite weak but still significant. It can be assumed that the bottom of the gap will be able to provide stronger relative gap support.
