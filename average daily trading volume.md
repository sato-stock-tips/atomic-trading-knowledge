What a market maker aims to do is to try and create a market, right? Literally, in the name, to make a market. The best way to make a market is to move the price to the liquidity zones. The liquidity zones are where the orders are. Doesn't matter if it's a limit buy order or if it's a stop loss sell order. Any zones, price zones that have orders, liquidity zones.

So the more orders there are, the more volume and daily turnover there is, the market maker has less opportunity to anyhow move the price to hit the orders
