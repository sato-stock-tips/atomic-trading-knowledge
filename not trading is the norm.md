

1. **Daily Intention:** I start every trading day with one clear mindset — I don’t want to trade. Action is the exception, not the goal. This alone filters out most bad decisions.
    
2. **Five-Star Standard:** I only trade [[setups]] that are so clean, so aligned, that ignoring them would be a mistake. If I have to convince myself, it’s not a [[five star setups]]. 
    
3. **Clear Definition:** A great setup is defined before the market opens. Rules, structure, context. No improvisation in real time.
    
4. **Model Book Focus:** I keep a model book with my absolute best setups. That’s my reference. If it doesn’t look like those, I pass.
    
5. **Trade-by-Trade Filter:** Every potential trade gets the same question: is this truly five-star, or am I bored, impatient, or emotional?
    
6. **Present Moment:** When I consider a trade, I’m fully focused on this chart, this risk, this execution. No distractions. No rushing.
    
7. **Selective Activity:** Most days, doing nothing is the right decision. Capital is protected by waiting, not by trading more.
    
8. **Earn the Trade:** Only when an opportunity is impossible to ignore do I become active. That’s when the odds are stacked in my favor.
    
9. **Less Trades, Better Results:** Fewer trades, higher quality. That’s how consistency is built — not by constant action, but by disciplined restraint.

### Never trade because you’re bored

Trading is a losing action. The more you trade without an edge, the more you will lose. Most traders blow up because they can’t sit still. (Phantom)

### Never spend all day trading

There’s honestly not that much to do. Prepare your headspace and look through your watchlist pre-market. Monitor and follow through with your plan during regular trading hours. 1 hour a day max per market.
