## The Role of ATR% in Stock Selection

Average True Range (ATR%) is one of the most overlooked tools in stock selection. It tells you how much a stock typically moves each day in percentage terms. If you're looking for explosive trades, ATR% is critical. Too low and the stock can't deliver real gains. Too high and you risk getting shaken out. Here's how to use it.


1. **Definition:** ATR% is the average daily price movement of a stock, expressed as a percentage of its price. A $20 stock with a 6% ATR typically swings $1.20 per day.
    
2. **Why It Matters:** ATR% shows whether a stock has enough range to produce big winners. Without sufficient movement, your equity curve will crawl instead of compound.
    
3. **The Minimum Threshold:** Look for ATR% > 5%. Below that, the stock is often too slow to produce meaningful moves — even if the setup looks perfect.
    
4. **The Sweet Spot:** ATR% between 5% and 12% is ideal for swing trading. Big enough to generate +50% moves quickly, but not so wild that you get shaken out by noise.
    
5. **When It's Too High:** ATR% above 15–20% means extreme volatility. These names swing hard but often fail. Trade smaller size if you engage.
    
6. **Daily volume turnover:** Combine ATR% with $200M+ daily turnover. A high ATR% in a thin stock often means traps, not opportunities.
    
7. **Position Sizing:** Higher ATR% = smaller position size. A stock moving 12% daily doesn't need the same size as one moving 5%. Adjust risk, not rules.
    
8. **Theme Context:** In hot themes (AI, lithium, crypto), ATR% spikes naturally. Be aware: the volatility can boost returns, but also drawdowns.
    
9. **Trade Management:** Stocks with high ATR% often require [[wide stops]] (7-10%). If you set stops too tight, normal movement will take you out.
    
10. **Screening Tip:** Use ATR% or volatility as a first filter in your stock screening. It cuts out dead money stocks and keeps your list focused on names that can actually move.
    
11. **Big Picture:** ATR% isn't a setup by itself — but it ensures your setups have the fuel to work. Combine ATR% with [[volume]], structure, and fundamentals for the best results.
