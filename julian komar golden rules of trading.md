## **My Top 15 Golden Rules of Trading**

1. **Start small, scale in:** Buy in three steps (50/30/20). Add only if the stock is up 20% + and still rising.
    
2. **Risk comes first:** Use stop losses (max 7–10%), limit position size (max 35%) based on [[R-multiples]], and control total portfolio risk (max 5%).
    
3. **Wait for [[five star setups]]:** Patience pays. Only trade your best, proven setups — nothing less.
    
4. **Concentrate your capital:** Put >50% of your money in leading themes with real momentum.
    
5. **Trust your intuition:** Two decades in the market sharpen instincts. Ignoring them costs money.
    
6. **Trade with the trend:** 95% of good trades happen in clear uptrends. Stay aligned with the [[market trend model]] and [[when not to trade]].
    
7. **Ignore other traders:** Opinions distract. Stay focused on your own process.
    
8. **Block out news & politics:** Charts, fundamentals, and story analysis give you everything you need. Price is the information you need. Volume perhaps.
    
9. **No random trades:** Plan every trade. Spontaneous trades are always emotional and always a mistake.
    
10. **Follow your process:** Success is in execution, not chasing money. The outcome takes care of itself. [[Chan Trading Handbook (Must-read)]]
    
11. **Don’t trade constantly:** Cash is a position. Sit out when nothing aligns.
    
12. **Forget losses fast:** Learn, reset, and focus on the next 100 trades. Protect your mental energy.
    
13. **Analyze & journal daily:** Data reveals patterns. Journaling builds awareness. Do it every day.
    
14. **Drop opinions & targets:** Let the market decide. Use trailing stops, not fixed expectations.
    
15. **Keep learning forever:** Every cycle teaches. Stay open. Evolve with the market.
