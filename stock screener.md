Screening for strong stocks

Our system trades opening range breakouts and breakouts in general. This means limited time during trading. You need to prepare two lists that you will focus on in the initial hour of trading. The watchlist and the hotlist.

- Buying at all time highs provide the best returns than any other price points    

## Building a watchlist

The watchlist is populated through two scans, details below. The bare minimum I expect in the watchlist is high average true range (ATR% > 3%) and strong relative strength (RS > 96). Look through your watchlist every week to remove stocks that do not meet these criteria

### Qullamagie episodic pivot scan

- % change today > 7.5%
- Close > Yesterday’s high
- Volume in dollars (Turnover) > $100m
- Primary stocks only
- Last price > $7
- 20 day ADR% > 3.5%
- Exclude [[sector]]: Biotech and Pharmaceuticals
- Market cap > $300m

### [[RS rating]] scan [[relative strength]]

- Price > $12
- Exclude sectors: Biotech and Pharmaceuticals
- ATR (14) 1W >5%
- Price * Vol > 50m
- Relative volume > 0.5
- Market cap > $1bn
- ATR (14) > 3%
- RSI (14) 1W > 70
